parser — day-first dates read month first

This one covers 374 reports. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out flipped: 7 April becomes 4 July. It happens in the app, by email, and on the booking-v2 form. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint — booking-v3 tells people to type month first

This one covers 33 reports, all through the booking form of the three tenants on booking-v3. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser is not in the path, so the parser fix leaves these as they are. The fix is the hint, or the form’s reading of it.

shift — the day moves by one, the month stays

This one covers 5 reports, and they describe no flip at all. The meeting moves by one day: “Tuesday 12 May” in the invite, Monday 11 May in the calendar; a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto see different days for one meeting; an invite arrives before its booking. This points at time zones or DST, not at day and month order. The desk had already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but left them counted under parser.
